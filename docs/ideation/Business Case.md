# Business Case

## Proposed Project
FirePath

## Date Produced
October 2026

## Background
Wildfire seasons in Canada are worsening, with 2023 being the most destructive on record. While Canada has the Canadian Forest Fire Danger Rating System (CFFDRS) and robust fire equations, these tools are built strictly for fire crews and experts (e.g., FBP Go, REDapp, WindNinja). Regular citizens in fire-prone areas currently lack an accessible way to understand fire behaviour near their homes.

## Business Need/ Opportunity
There is a gap in the market for a public-facing, easy-to-use tool that translates complex Canadian fire science into visual map data. Cabin owners, farmers, and campers currently have to dig through complex government websites and decipher codes. FirePath bridges this gap by combining free Canadian data with an intuitive map interface, providing clear, plain-language fire danger scenarios.

## Options
- **Option 1: Do Nothing.** Rely on existing government websites and professional tools.
- **Option 2: Build FirePath.** Develop a custom map application that automates the data pipeline and simplifies the Canadian fire equations for the public.
- **Option 3: Modify Existing Tools.** Attempt to adapt US-based tools (like Wildfire Analyst Pocket) for Canadian data, which is constrained by proprietary code. Not to mention that the tools are not designed for Canadian environment.

## Cost-Benefit Analysis
- **Option 1:** 
  - *Costs:* Continued confusion and lack of preparedness for rural Canadians; potential loss of property or safety risks. 
  - *Benefits:* No development effort required.
- **Option 2 (FirePath):** 
  - *Costs:* Team development time, requirement to build and maintain a daily data cleaning pipeline. 
  - *Benefits:* Directly addresses the Canadian market, uses official data, provides immense value to non-professional users, highly scalable (MVP 1 to MVP 3).
- **Option 3:** 
  - *Costs:* High effort to reverse-engineer or license proprietary tools. 
  - *Benefits:* Potentially faster deployment if a partnership were possible (unlikely for a capstone).

## Recommendation
Proceeding with **Option 2 (Build FirePath)** will deliver a simple, yet impactful solution for Canadians living with wildfire risk.
