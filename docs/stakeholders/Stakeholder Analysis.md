# Stakeholder Analysis

**Project Name:** FirePath

| **Name** | **Project Role** | **Power** | **Interest** | **Level of Support** |
|-----------|------------------|------------|---------------|-----------------------|
| Public users | Cabin owners, farmers, campers and travellers who would use the app to see how a wildfire would behave at a spot they care about | Low | High | Neutral |
| Communities | Small towns, rural municipalities and volunteer fire departments who could use the app for their area and share it with residents | High | High | Neutral |
| Dr. Tim Maciag | Course instructor and project sponsor who approves our scope and reviews our work | High | High | Supportive |
| SPSA and SaskAlert | Saskatchewan's wildfire agency and emergency alert system. The app points users to them for official alerts | High | Low | Neutral |
| Government data providers | Natural Resources Canada, Environment and Climate Change Canada and NASA, who publish the data the app runs on | High | Low | Neutral |
