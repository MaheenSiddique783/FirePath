# Stakeholder Management Plan

**Project Name:** FirePath

| **Name** | **About the Stakeholder** | **Plan** |
|-----------|----------------------------|-----------|
| Public users | The people the app is built for. They aren't fire experts, so results need to be in plain language. We haven't interviewed any yet. | Interview a few people from each user group to check our assumptions. Run usability tests on the prototype and adjust the app based on what we learn. |
| Communities | Local groups that could use the app for their whole area and could become pilot partners. We haven't contacted any yet. | Reach out to at least one rural municipality or volunteer fire department to learn what they would need, and ask if they'd be willing to try the app. |
| Dr. Tim Maciag | Our course instructor. He suggested building a Canadian version of Wildfire Analyst Pocket. | Bring questions to each workshop, keep GitHub and the Kanban board up to date, and tell him early about any change in scope. |
| SPSA and SaskAlert | The official sources for wildfire information and emergency alerts in Saskatchewan. The app must never contradict them. | Show on every result that the app is not an evacuation tool, and link users to SaskAlert and SPSA. Use the official danger levels instead of making up our own. |
| Government data providers | The app depends on their data, so if a data feed changes, the app breaks. | Follow their licence and credit rules, download data on a schedule instead of on every request, and watch for changes to their feeds. |
