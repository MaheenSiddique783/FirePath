# Business Model Analysis

An app that lets regular people in Canada tap any spot and see how a wildfire would behave there, using Canada's own fire science and free government data. This is our plan for how it would be offered.
### What is offered

* Map app where you tap any spot
* How fast a fire would spread there and which way, drawn as a shape on the map
* The official danger level with a plain English explanation
* Saved places with alerts when the danger changes or a new fire is detected nearby
* Active fires, fire history around a spot and a smoke layer on the same map

### Value Proposition

* Takes the science fire agencies already use and makes it something anyone can read
* Fills in the vegetation, slope, weather and wind by itself, so nobody has to know what a fuel type is
* Shows direction and distance, not just a danger rating
* Works year round through fire history and saved places, not only in fire season
* The safety information stays free

### To whom it is offered

* People who aren't fire professionals but live with wildfire risk
    * Cabin and cottage owners
    * Farmers and ranchers
    * Campers, hikers and travellers
* Small towns, rural municipalities and volunteer fire departments
* Campgrounds, tourism boards and park operators
* Not fire crews, and not an evacuation tool. The app always points people to official alerts

### How it is offered

* A web app that works on phones and computers, so people can check it at the cabin, on the farm or at the campsite
* People would find it through search, social media and links from towns and parks

### Why we capture value

* A free app builds a base of people who trust it, and every other idea below depends on that trust
* Towns and RMs are likely to value something that saves staff time and helps them warn residents sooner
* Campgrounds and tourism operators get fire safety information their guests can trust without building it themselves
* Governments already fund wildfire preparedness, so a free tool that helps communities get ready may fit those programs. We'd still need to find the specific ones

### Alternatives

| Alternative                                     | Shortcoming                                                  | Where FirePath wins                                      |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Wildfire Analyst Pocket                         | Built for firefighters and uses US data and US fire equations | Canadian equations and Canadian data                     |
| FBP Go and REDapp                               | Built for agency staff, with no map or live weather in FBP Go | Map, live weather and no fuel codes to type in           |
| CWFIS and provincial websites                   | Index numbers and fuel codes that most people can't read     | Same official danger levels, explained in plain words    |
| Weather apps                                    | Show wind and temperature but not what a fire would do       | Turns the weather into a fire spread shape               |

### Revenue Model

* Free public app for the core features
 wildfire and emergency preparedness programs
